FLORIDA LLC ANNUAL REPORTS

Stay covered every year.

Florida LLCs must file an Annual Report each year beginning in the calendar year after formation. Our Annual Compliance Plan is designed to make that yearly obligation easier to manage.

Jan 1
Annual-report filing opensFlorida opens the annual-report filing window at the beginning of each calendar year.

Our recommended targetWe aim to have customers handled before the last-minute rush.
May 1
Deadline to avoid the late feeFlorida assesses a $400 late fee after May 1.
September
Administrative-dissolution riskEntities that still have not filed can face administrative dissolution later in September.

QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU START

Clear answers before checkout.

What does Form Florida do?

Form Florida helps entrepreneurs prepare and submit Florida business formation filings through a guided online process. FormFlorida.com is owned and operated by Form Florida LLC.

Is Form Florida part of Sunbiz or the State of Florida?

No. Form Florida is an independent business formation service and is not affiliated with the Florida Department of State, Division of Corporations, Sunbiz, the IRS, or any government agency. Customers can also file directly with the government.

Is Form Florida a law firm?

No. Form Florida does not provide legal, tax, accounting, investment, or financial advice. Individualized questions should be directed to a qualified professional.

How will I receive my order?

This is a digital service. After the requested filing is prepared and submitted, status updates and available documents are delivered electronically to the email address associated with your order. Government processing time is outside our control.

Can I cancel or request a refund?

Cancellation and refund eligibility depends on whether work or a government submission has begun. Review the Refund, Cancellation & Dispute Policy before purchasing.

Are prices in U.S. dollars?

Yes. All prices shown on this website and charged through checkout are in USD.
